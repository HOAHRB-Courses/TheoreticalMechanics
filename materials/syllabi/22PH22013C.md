# 理论力学（中外合作）教学大纲

- 课程代码：22PH22013C
- 学分：6
- 总学时：216
- 讲授学时：64
- 答疑学时：152
- 来源版本日期：2024.12

## 教学内容

| 序号 | 教学内容 | 教学要求 | 学时 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction to classical mechanics. The concepts of a particle, a system of particles, a rigid body. Space and time in classical mechanics. Frames of reference. The limits of applicability of classical mechanics. | Understand the basic concepts and models of classical mechanics | 2 |
| 2 | Kinematics of a particle: radius vector, velocity, acceleration, various coordinate systems (Cartesian, cylindrical, spherical). The law of motion and the equation of the trajectory. Velocity and acceleration in Frenet-Serret frame. Curvature and torsion of the trajectory, the Frenet-Serret formulas. Areal velocity. Motion with a constant areal velocity, the Binet equation. | Master kinematics of a particle in various coordinate systems | 2 |
| 3 | The concept of force and mass, Newton's laws. Galileo's principle of relativity, Galilean transformation. Examples of solving the equations of motion in special cases: a force that depends only on time and a force that depends only on velocity. | Understand the Newton’s laws and Galileo's principle of relativity. | 2 |
| 4 | Solution of the equations of one-dimensional motion in the case of a force that depends only on the coordinate: separation of variables, energy conservation. Study of one-dimensional motion of a particle (bounded and unbounded motion, potential well and barrier, turning points, period of oscillation). | Master the one-dimensional motion of a particle when the force depends on the coordinates only. | 2 |
| 5 | Variation of linear momentum, angular momentum and energy of a particle over time. The concept of constants of motion. Work and mechanical power. Potential, gyroscopic and dissipative forces. The relationship between the force and potential energy. Scaling transformations. | Understand the concept of motion constants. | 2 |
| 7 | Variation of linear momentum, angular momentum and energy of a system of particles over time. The center of mass of the system of particles, its motion, external and internal forces. Kinetic energy, potential energy and total mechanical energy. An isolated system, the conservation of its energy in the absence of dissipative forces. | Master the equations for variation of momentum, angular momentum and energy of the system of particles. | 2 |
| 6 | The virial of Clausius and virial theorem, the case of a homogeneous function as the potential energy. | Master the virial theorem. | 2 |
| 8 | The two-body problem, its reduction to the problem of the motion of a particle in a central force field, the motion of the center of mass and relative motion; reduced mass. Motion in a central force field, conservation of the angular momentum and energy, effective potential energy. | Master the reduction of a two-body problem to a single-particle problem in a central force field. | 2 |
| 9 | Motion in a central force field, orbital plane, equation of the trajectory in polar coordinates and the laws of motion. Investigation of possible trajectories, bounded and unbounded motion, circular orbits. Conditions for closed orbits, Bertrand’s theorem, falling to the center. | Master the laws of motion and trajectory equations in a central force field. | 2 |
| 10 | The Kepler problem: deriving the orbit equation in canonical form, types of orbits, Kepler's laws, time dependence of the distance from the center. The case of repulsive force. The Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector. | Understand Keplers’s laws. Master the orbit equations. | 2 |
| 11 | Particle scattering in a central force field: impact parameter, scattering angle, differential and total scattering cross-section. Scattering of charged particles by a Coulomb field, the Rutherford scattering cross section. | Understand the concept of the differential and total cross section. Master the Rutherford cross section. | 2 |
| 12 | Rainbow scattering and glory scattering. Elastic scattering of two particles in the center-of-mass and laboratory frames. | Master the elastic scattering of two particles in the  laboratory frames. | 2 |
| 13 | The motion of the system of particles with constraints. Classification of constraints. Holonomic ideal constraints, generalized coordinates. The principle of virtual work and d'Alembert’s principle. Lagrange’s equations of the second kind, their derivation from d'Alembert’s principle. | Master Lagrange’s equations of the second kind. | 2 |
| 14 | The Lagrangian. Friction forces and Rayleigh’s dissipation function. Generalization of Lagrange’s equations to nonholonomic constraints. Lagrange’s equations of the first kind (with constraint forces). | Understand Rayleigh’s dissipation function and the concept of Lagrange multipliers. | 2 |
| 15 | The principle of least action (the Hamilton – Ostrogradsky principle), the derivation of Lagrange’s equations of the second kind from this principle. The Lagrangian and conservation laws, generalized momenta, cyclic coordinates. Noether's theorem. | Understand the relationship between the symmetries and motion constants | 2 |
| 16 | Free small vibrations in the one-dimensional case. The position of stable equilibrium. Approximation of the kinetic and potential energy. The frequency of free vibration. Damped vibrations in the presence of friction. Forced one-dimensional oscillations. The case of sinusoidal dependence of the driving force on time. The resonance. Forced vibrations in the presence of friction. | Master the one-dimensional free and forced vibrations. | 2 |
| 17 | Free small vibrations in systems with many degrees of freedom. The position of stable equilibrium. Matrices of the kinetic and potential energy coefficients. Approximation of the Lagrange function in a vicinity of the equilibrium position. The equations of motion and their solution. Vibration frequencies and vectors. Degenerate vibration modes. Normal coordinates. Transition to normal coordinates in the Lagrange function. Forced oscillations in multidimensional systems. | Master free oscillations in multidimensional systems. Understand the concept of normal coordinates. | 2 |
| 18 | The kinematics of a rigid body. Description of rotations: direction cosines, Euler angles. Euler's theorem on the arbitrary motion of a rigid body with one fixed point. Chasles's theorem. Angular velocity and its properties. Euler’s kinematic equations. | Understand the definitions of the Euler angles, master Euler’s kinematic equations. | 2 |
| 19 | Motion in a noninertial frame of reference. Velocity and acceleration in a rotating frame. The rotational, Coriolis and centripetal accelerations. | Master the velocity and acceleration in a rotating frame. | 2 |
| 20 | Dynamics of a rigid body: rotational kinetic energy, inertia tensor, principal axes and principal moments of inertia, inertia ellipsoid. Symmetrical top, ball top, rotator. Steiner theorem. Angular momentum of a rigid body, its relationship with the angular velocity. | Master the kinetic energy and angular momentum of a rigid body. | 2 |
| 21 | Euler’s equations of motion for a rigid body with one fixed point. Torque-free motion of a rigid body, Poinsot’s construction. Steady free rotation of an asymmetric top and its stability.  Regular precession of a free symmetrical top. | Master Euler’s equations of motion, understand Poinsot’s construction. | 2 |
| 22 | Heavy symmetrical top with one fixed point: Lagrange’s equations and their solution. Constants of motion and their physical meaning. | Master Lagrange’s equations for a heavy symmetrical top with one fixed point. | 2 |
| 23 | Heavy symmetrical top with one fixed point. The motion in detail: irregular precession, nutation, natural initial conditions. Fast spinning top. Sleeping top. | Describe the motion of a heavy symmetrical top with one fixed point in detail. | 2 |
| 24 | Hamilton’s canonical equations of motion, derivation using the Legendre transformation. The Hamiltonian. Examples: Hamiltonians for a particle in Cartesian and spherical coordinates. Conservation of generalized energy. Phase space. Liouville's theorem on conservation of phase volume. The Lagrangian and Hamiltonian for a charged particle in an electromagnetic field. | Master Hamilton’s canonical equations. | 2 |
| 25 | The Poisson brackets and their properties. Fundamental Poisson brackets. Jacobi’s identity. The Poisson brackets and constants of motion, Poisson’s theorem. The Poincare – Cartan integral invariant (the principal integral invariant of mechanics), the Poincare integral. | Understand the Poisson brackets, master the Poincare – Cartan integral invariant. | 2 |
| 26 | Canonical transformations. Generating functions of various types. Examples of canonical transformations: identical transformation, replacing coordinates with momenta and momenta with coordinates, point transformation. Canonical transformation to the cyclic coordinates for a harmonic oscillator. | Understand the concept of canonical transformations. | 2 |
| 27 | Action as a function of coordinates and time. Hamilton’s principal function. The Hamilton – Jacobi equation, its complete solution, Jacobi’s theorem. | Master the Hamilton – Jacobi equation. | 2 |
| 28 | Separation of variables in the Hamilton – Jacobi equation: the case of the time-independent Hamiltonian, the case of cyclic coordinates, other cases of separation of variables. Example: motion in a field of a central force. Briefly about the separation of variables in spheroidal and parabolic coordinate systems. | Understand the separation of variables technique for solving the Hamilton – Jacobi equation. | 2 |
| 29 | Abbreviated action. The variational principle of Maupertuis. Jacobi’s principle. The case of a particle moving along a curved surface in the absence of external forces, geodesic lines. | Master the integral variational principles. | 2 |
| 30 | Periodic motions such as libration and rotation, corresponding phase trajectories, separatrices. Example: a simple pendulum. Multiperiodic and true periodic motions, cases of absence and presence of degeneracy. Action – angle variables, calculation of oscillation frequencies. Example: anisotropic spatial oscillator. | Understand the concept of action -angle variables. | 2 |
| 31 | The Kepler problem in action-angle variables. The action quantization rule (Bohr-Sommerfeld quantization rule), an equation for the energy levels of a hydrogen atom. | Master the Kepler problem in action-angle variables. | 2 |
| 32 | Adiabatic invariants. Example: small oscillations of a variable-length pendulum. | Understand the concept of adiabatic invariance of the action variable. | 2 |
